Featured · M.Sc. Thesis 2000 – 2024 Solo

Cryo-Hydrological Water Security Index & Vertical Displacement of Water — Upper Ganga Basin, Central Himalaya

A distributed, SPHY-inspired machine-learning water-balance framework to assess cryosphere change and water security across the Central Himalaya, and a new concept to expose the elevation mismatch between where water is generated and where it is consumed.

  • Assessed cryosphere change & water security across an 18,764 km² basin using remote sensing and GIS.
  • Developed the CHWSI and a distributed ML + SPHY-based water-balance model to quantify water availability, demand and stress.
  • Identified 34.6% glacier loss and an 878 m elevation gap between water generation and consumption.
  • Proposed the Vertical Displacement of Water concept to highlight the generation–consumption elevation mismatch.

Spatio-Temporal Dynamics of the Urban Heat Island — Delhi

Multi-temporal UHI analysis linking land-cover change to surface temperature across two decades.

  • Processed Landsat 5/7/8 thermal imagery in GEE — radiometric calibration, cloud masking, co-registration & LST retrieval.

  • Ran multi-temporal UHI intensity, spatial-pattern & change-detection analysis with Pearson correlation.
