Featured · M.Sc. Thesis ✦ Predictive Modelling 2000 – 2030 Solo

Cryo-Hydrological Water Security & Vertical Displacement of Water Generation — Upper Ganga Basin

A distributed cryosphere–hydrology framework quantifying glacier retreat, snow dynamics and water security across the Upper Ganga Basin — exposing the vertical mismatch between where water is generated and where it is consumed.

  • Processed multi-temporal Landsat Surface Reflectance in Google Earth Engine with NDSI-based glacier mapping to track glacier extent across 2000, 2014 & 2024.
  • Derived seasonal Snow Cover Frequency from MODIS MOD10A1 via NDSI thresholding & pixel-level QA screening.
  • Fused glacier extent & snow cover through normalization and weighted overlay into a Cryosphere Health Score (CHS).
  • Built a distributed SPHY-based hydrological water-balance model over 6,393 grid cells — precipitation, temperature lapse rate, snow/ice melt, soil moisture & evapotranspiration — to estimate spatial water availability.
  • Integrated water demand & stress ratios with cryosphere indicators into the Cryo-Hydrological Water Security Index (CHWSI), then analysed vertical generation–demand separation while projecting basin conditions to 2030.

Spatio-Temporal Dynamics of the Urban Heat Island — Delhi (2016–2024)

Multi-sensor analysis of Delhi's thermal–vegetation–water restructuring and thermal-risk zoning.

  • Characterised urban surface dynamics from multi-temporal Sentinel-2 imagery, capturing vegetation, built-up & surface-water change.
  • Quantified transformation via NDVI, NDBI & NDWI, revealing Delhi's urban–vegetation–water restructuring.
  • Mapped LULC transitions & urban expansion through multi-temporal classification and change-detection (2016 → 2024).
  • Investigated thermal behaviour via Land Surface Temperature & Apparent Thermal Inertia, then integrated NDVI, NDBI & LST into a spatial Urban Thermal Vulnerability Index (UTVI) to delineate thermal-risk zones.

✦ ML & Geostatistics Chambal Basin LJMU, UK · Ongoing

Surface Water–Groundwater Interaction under Climatic & Anthropogenic Forcing — Chambal River Basin

A multi-source geospatial–geostatistical model of river–aquifer exchange and recharge prioritisation, in collaboration with Liverpool John Moores University, UK.

  • Fused a multi-resolution raster stack — SRTM 30 m DEM, Copernicus/ESRI Sentinel-2 land cover, ERA5, SoilGrids & GLHYMPS — to a common 1 km grid (UTM 44N); derived drainage products in Arc Hydro.
  • Ran post-classification change detection on a 25-year land-cover series, quantifying 353.7% built-up expansion via transition matrices.
  • Modelled river–aquifer exchange via raster algebra — water-balance recharge, Baseflow Index & Darcy flux (q = −T∇h) over potentiometric surfaces from Empirical Bayesian Kriging of CGWB wells.
  • Mapped per-pixel trends & anomalies with Mann–Kendall & Sen's slope across 25 annual layers, separating climatic from anthropogenic forcing (mean BFI 0.30 → 0.60).
  • Built an AHP-MCDA weighted-overlay suitability model (11 layers, CR < 0.10) to prioritise groundwater-recharge zones.
