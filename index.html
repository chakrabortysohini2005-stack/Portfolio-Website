Cryo-Hydrological Water Security Index & Vertical Displacement of Water Generation — Upper Ganga Basin

A predictive water-security risk framework for the Upper Ganga Basin — a region serving 50M+ downstream residents — fusing multi-temporal satellite imagery with deep-learning hydrological modelling to forecast glacier-driven water stress.

  • Fused multi-temporal satellite imagery with spatial analytics to quantify glacier retreat & seasonal snow-cover variability through a custom Cryosphere Health Score (CHS).
  • Engineered a scalable, distributed hydrological-simulation machine-learning model across 6,393 grid cells, integrating climate, terrain, soil & evapotranspiration data into spatially explicit water-availability estimates for resource-planning stakeholders.
  • Built a time-series forecasting pipeline (advanced statistics) projecting water-stress scenarios to 2030, translating complex multi-source data into actionable decision-support indicators & risk dashboards, enabling policymakers to prioritise climate-resilient interventions across high-risk sub-basins.

Spatio-Temporal Dynamics of the Urban Heat Island — Delhi (2016–2024)

Multi-sensor UHI analysis mapping Delhi's land-cover change to zone-level thermal-risk hotspots.

  • Processed 8 years of multi-temporal imagery in Google Earth Engine — spectral indicators (NDVI, NDBI, NDWI) with supervised classification & post-classification change detection to map land-use transitions & urban expansion.
  • Built a composite Urban Thermal Vulnerability Index (UTVI) from land-surface temperature, land cover & spectral indicators — zone-level heat-risk maps tied to impervious-surface growth for targeted cooling & green-infrastructure investment.
  • Delivered a replicable, scalable workflow linking satellite-derived urban change to thermal-risk outcomes — a methodology template deployable to any Indian metro.

✦ ML & Geostatistics Chambal Basin LJMU, UK · Ongoing

Surface Water–Groundwater Interaction under Climatic & Anthropogenic Forcing — Chambal River Basin

A multi-source geospatial–geostatistical model of river–aquifer exchange and recharge prioritisation, in collaboration with Liverpool John Moores University, UK.

  • Fused a multi-resolution raster stack — SRTM 30 m DEM, Copernicus CCI & ESRI Sentinel-2 land cover, ERA5, SoilGrids & GLHYMPS — to a 1 km grid (UTM 44N); derived Arc Hydro drainage products as infiltration controls.
  • Ran post-classification change detection on a 25-year land-cover series, quantifying 353.7% built-up expansion via transition matrices.
  • Modelled river–aquifer exchange via raster algebra — water-balance recharge, Baseflow Index & Darcy flux (q = −T∇h) — over potentiometric surfaces from Empirical Bayesian Kriging of CGWB wells.
  • Mapped trends with Mann–Kendall & Sen's slope across 25 layers (mean BFI 0.30 → 0.60) and built an AHP-MCDA suitability model (11 layers, CR < 0.10) to prioritise recharge zones.
